BLINK — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Welcome to BLINK. We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning information collection, use, and disclosure in the BLINK mobile application.

1. Information Collection & Storage

BLINK does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).

  • Offline Local Storage: All user game records, cosmic realm shifts, unlocked relics, high scores, and energy levels are saved solely on your local device.
  • No User Accounts: No sign-up, registration, or external authentication is required.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional on-device local notifications for energy recharge and daily reward reminders.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Provides tactile haptic feedback during interactive cosmic shifts.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used exclusively to retrieve dynamic UI typography (Google Fonts). No private information is sent across the network.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Allows the local notification scheduler to restore reminders after device reboot.

3. Third-Party Services

BLINK does not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or invasive analytics. Application delivery and telemetry are provided by the Google Play Store under Google's standard privacy guidelines.

4. Children's Privacy

BLINK contains no harmful content and is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, BLINK is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

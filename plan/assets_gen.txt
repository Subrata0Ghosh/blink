Create a complete ORIGINAL visual asset pack for a premium mobile game called **BLINK — The World Changes When You Look Away**.

IMPORTANT:

* All designs must be completely original.
* Do NOT copy characters, environments, UI, logos, effects, shapes, compositions, colors, or visual assets from Clash of Clans, Subway Surfers, Candy Crush, Monument Valley, Pokémon, or any other existing game.
* Use only the general quality level of modern premium mobile games as inspiration.
* The visual identity must be recognizable as its own IP.
* Use a polished mobile-game aesthetic suitable for Google Play Store.
* Assets must be consistent with each other in perspective, lighting, proportions, materials, and art direction.

# Privacy Policy for BLINK

**Last Updated:** September 25, 2026

Welcome to **BLINK** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BLINK mobile application (the "App").

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**BLINK does not collect, store, or share any personal information or personally identifiable data (PII).**

- **Gameplay & Progress Data:** All game progress, cosmic shift achievements, energy states, relics, and settings are saved locally on your device via standard local preferences (`SharedPreferences`). None of this information is transmitted to our servers or third-party servers.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address or phone number to play BLINK.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Used

The App requests the following minimal permissions strictly to enable in-game features:

1. **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**:
   - Used exclusively to send local on-device atmospheric reminders (e.g., energy recharged, daily rewards ready).
   - No notification tracking or remote push token is sent over the network.
   - You can disable notifications at any time in the in-game Settings or your device system settings.

2. **Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)**:
   - Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., button clicks, realm shifts, streak rewards).

3. **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**:
   - Used for loading dynamic typography fonts (`Google Fonts`) and basic connectivity verification. The App does not collect or transmit user data over the internet.

4. **Boot Completed (`android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`)**:
   - Used to reschedule pending local reminders if your device is restarted.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

- **No Third-Party Trackers:** BLINK does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or user behavior tracking suites (e.g., no Facebook SDK, no AdMob, no third-party telemetry).
- **Google Play Services:** The App is distributed through Google Play Store, which may collect standard, anonymized system diagnostic and crash telemetry as governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

BLINK is designed for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is requested or collected, the App complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

---

### 5. Data Security

Because your gameplay data remains strictly on your local device, the security of your data is governed by your device's built-in operating system security measures.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
